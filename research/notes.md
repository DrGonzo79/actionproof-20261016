# Source research — ActionProof

- **Report:** “Reconciliation tool that catches AI support mistakes for ecommerce brands”
- **Date / source:** 2026-10-16, [IdeaBrowser Idea of the Day](https://www.ideabrowser.com/hub/ideas/today), read from the authenticated rendered page.
- **Headline indicators:** IB Index 7.3; SaaS; $5M–$12M ARR ceiling; moderate difficulty; $5K–$25K starting capital; first revenue in 60–90 days. These are report estimates, not verified outcomes.
- **Buyer:** Head of CX / support operations at a $2M+ ARR ecommerce brand with thousands of monthly tickets and autonomous refund, return, cancellation, or subscription actions.
- **Painful status quo:** teams sample conversations, compare tickets to Shopify/Stripe/Recharge manually, discover mistakes through complaints, and apologize after the fact. A low-cost ops analyst plus spreadsheet is the real competitor.
- **Wedge:** independent, read-only, exception-only reconciliation. Compare the agent’s explicit promise to the final system-of-record event; do not become another support agent or generic chat grader.
- **Why now:** report cites 2,000+ Gorgias AI Agent adopters in under a year and 35–60% autonomous closure on configured stores. It also cites a Cloud Security Alliance survey where 53% saw agents exceed permissions and a Ravelin survey where 44% of merchants were integrating agentic protocols. These are directional signals and require source verification before investor use.
- **Evidence / demand:** operator quotes describe wrong amounts, wrong entities, invented policies, and errors requiring apologies. Gorgias reportedly serves 15,000+ brands, including 40% of the top 1,500 Shopify stores. Direct search demand is thin: “Gorgias AI” 210/mo, “Siena AI” 720/mo, “Gorgias alternative” 110/mo; problem terms are near zero. Outbound/partners matter more than SEO.
- **Competitors:** ResponseCX ($4,250–$5,000/mo base plus outcome fees) is both validator and closest threat; Gorgias, Siena ($750/mo + usage), Yuma ($850–$1,200/mo), and Kustomer own agent workflows; Provy is horizontal outcome assurance; MaestroQA is conversation QA. Native verification from Gorgias/Shopify/Stripe is the existential risk.
- **Offer ladder:** free 30-day retroactive audit → Starter Reconciliation $1,500/mo (5,000 tickets) → Growth $3,500/mo (25,000 tickets, Recharge, rules, digest) → $2,000/quarter ops review. Hook: return an exception list with order IDs and dollar impact.
- **Economics:** report model assumes 300 year-one conversations, 25% audit acceptance, 20% audit-to-paid, 15 customers, and $2,500 blended MRR; that implies $37.5K exit MRR and ~$225K booked year-one revenue. Every conversion input is unproven.
- **GTM:** Gorgias/Shopify agencies and CX operators first; founder-led outbound second. The report suggests 500 cold DMs/month and notes Gorgias partner economics, but partner terms must be reconfirmed.
- **Biggest risks:** native bundling, ResponseCX adding read-only mode, intermittent pain failing to earn budget, 9-month enterprise cycles, integration/API drift, false positives, and exposure of sensitive customer/order data.
- **Build direction:** deterministic reconciliation rules first; optional LLM extraction only for mapping unstructured ticket language into a typed promise. The prototype uses explicit fictional fixtures and no external APIs.
- **Source domains named by the report:** Cloud Security Alliance, Ravelin, Ecommerce Times, Gorgias, MyAskAI, G2, Ringly, Eesel, ClickPost, Reddit, Shopify. Raw newsletter text and private tracking links are intentionally excluded.
